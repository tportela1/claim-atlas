# Claim Atlas product brief

People miss settlements because notices never arrive, activity is hard to remember, and filing/payment records are scattered. The product discovers potential opportunities, explains evidence and uncertainty, and keeps the next step visible.

## Journeys

1. Choose notice-first or public-first; select connected mail accounts. Explicit requests skip answered questions.
2. Notice-first finds invitations and follow-ups over the prior 12 months by default.
3. Public-first enumerates directories/community leads, verifies official rules, then searches mail over each class period.
4. Review supported, unknown and contradicted criteria. Personal confirmation can fill gaps; forwarded and marketing mail alone cannot establish use.
5. Independently decide whether to file, then explicitly record filing, enrollment, watch status or payment. Refresh preserves decisions.

## Acceptance criteria

Generic discovery offers exactly two routes. Selected accounts are honored. Cases absent from notice mail can still match. Pending lawsuits never become payable claims. Directory duplicates merge by case, not company. Blocked/truncated coverage stays partial. Filing actions require dated official verification and supported material criteria. Follow-ups reference tracked owners. Offline reports safely escape hostile content, wrap on mobile and retain payments. Installs preserve reports/trackers and packages exclude private data.

## Product surface

Portable instructions, provider-adaptive research, public-only query helper, deterministic report renderer, local tracker contract, three distribution formats, installer and fictitious demo. Mail/web access comes from the host. This is not a hosted claims marketplace, automatic filer or central purchase-history database. Community contributions are reviewed public leads, not a hosted crowdsourcing service.

Compact top navigation leads into a cobalt briefing header with citrus/coral accents and light evidence cards; navigation wraps on mobile. Evidence, missing facts and next steps are separate; no aggregate payout promise. Next actions, matches, verification, tracking/payments, watchlist, closed windows, security and coverage all have stable destinations.

Release checks exercise renderer behavior, public inventory, package parity/privacy, installer preservation and rollback. Fixture checks do not certify live provider coverage or legal eligibility. Future contributions can add documented provider adapters, public-source fixtures and localization.
